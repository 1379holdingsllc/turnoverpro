Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how TurnoverPro, a service of 1379 Holdings LLC (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and shares information when you use the TurnoverPro website at https://1379holdingsllc.github.io/turnoverpro/ and the TurnoverPro Android app. The same policy applies to both the website and the Android app version.

Who we are

TurnoverPro is operated by 1379 Holdings LLC. TurnoverPro connects customers with independent providers. Contact: 1379holdingsllc@gmail.com, call/text (470) 279-6694.

Information we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms and send to us. We do not use GPS or device location, we do not require an account, and we do not collect contacts, photos or files from your device.

Request forms may include:

  • Name, phone number and email address
  • City & ZIP code
  • The service or item you need, and the job details you describe
  • Preferred date and whether your date/time is flexible
  • Whether it is OK to text you about the request
  • Budget or price range (optional)
  • Bedrooms / bathrooms

Provider / listing forms may include:

  • Name, phone number and email address
  • City or service area you cover
  • What you offer, your experience and availability
  • Whether it is OK to text you about listing opportunities
  • Your rates or prices and a link to photos (optional)

Each form also sends which TurnoverPro section it came from. If you call, text or email us, we receive what you send that way, including your phone number or email address.

Cookies, analytics and ads

We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising on this site. We do not load third-party fonts or scripts. Photos are stored on and served from our own site (they came from Pexels and Unsplash, which are credited in the footer; those links only open their sites if you tap them).

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to operate the service. We do not use those logs to identify or track you.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request or listing and follow up with questions
  • To give you a price or quote and confirm details
  • To match your request with an independent provider who can do the job or rental
  • To send invoices and keep business records
  • To call or email you about your request. We text you only if you checked “OK to text” or texted us first, and you can ask us to stop at any time (reply STOP)

How we share information

We never sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) processes form submissions and delivers them to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • PayPal is used to send invoices and take payment once a booking is confirmed. PayPal handles payment details under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card number.
  • The independent provider doing your job or rental receives the details needed to do it (for example your name, contact details, location and job details). For provider listings, we may share relevant listing details with customers.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to handle your request or listing and for normal business records (such as invoices), and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see below).

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, so information you send through the forms is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit access to your information to what is needed to provide the service.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop texting or calling you. Email 1379holdingsllc@gmail.com (or call/text (470) 279-6694) with your request and the name and phone or email you used. We respond within 30 days. Records we must keep for legal or tax reasons may be retained only as long as required.

Children

TurnoverPro is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
